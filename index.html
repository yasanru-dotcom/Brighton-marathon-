Strength, recovery and fuelling

Strength A — Monday: 20–30 minutes of controlled single-leg work, calf raises, glute/hip exercises and trunk stability.

Strength B — Friday: 15–25 minutes of lighter posterior-chain, calf and core exercises. Avoid heavy lifting that leaves your legs sore for Sunday.

Long runs: Practise your race-day carbohydrate, fluid and gel plan. Test everything in training.

Important: This is a guide, not a requirement to run seven days every week. Take optional rest days, do not make up missed mileage, and reduce training if fatigue or pain accumulates. Stop if pain changes your running stride. Weekly totals are approximate; individual day distances can be adjusted.
